what is puspose of this data set?
The purpose of this dataset is to analyze and understand the relationship between experimental conditions and student performance while handling missing data.
how missing values were handled?
issing values in the dataset were handled using mean imputation, where missing experiment scores were replaced by the average value of the available data .
why that strategy was choosen?
Mean imputation was chosen because it is a simple and effective method for handling missing numerical values, preserving dataset size while maintaining overall data consistency.
what visualisations were generated?
The dataset was visualized using line plots and scatter plots for student_id vs temperature, along with histograms, bar charts, and box plots to analyze score distribution, compare experiments, and observe the impact of missing value imputation.
what are the limitations are?
The dataset has limitations such as small size, reduced variability due to mean imputation, limited features, and weak insights from using student_id as a variable. These factors may affect the accuracy and reliability of analysis.
