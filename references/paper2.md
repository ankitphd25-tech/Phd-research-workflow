Abstract: This survey presents a structured and accessible review of Quantum Machine Learning (QML), with the aim of introducing new readers to its fundamental concepts while also covering recent research developments. We review key paradigms including Quantum Neural Networks (QNNs), Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks (QCNNs), and hybrid quantum–classical architectures. Quantum Machine Learning (QML) is an emerging field that combines quantum computing and machine learning, leveraging qubits that can exist in superposition and exhibit entanglement to enable computational capabilities beyond those of classical systems. These properties allow quantum models to explore solution spaces in parallel, potentially offering advantages in speed or efficiency for certain tasks. We analyze theoretical benefits, practical limitations, and emerging applications of QML in areas such as medical AI, cryptography, and optimization. Furthermore, we discuss key challenges related to quantum hardware scalability, noise resilience, and training inefficiencies. The paper concludes with a roadmap for future research, emphasizing progress in error mitigation, quantum data encoding, and hybrid deep learning integration.
Keywords: Quantum Computing; Quantum Machine Learning; Quantum Neural Networks; QCNNs; Hybrid Quantum Models
